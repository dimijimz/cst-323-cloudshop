Stock you can trust

Each purchase checks and reduces stock in a single database statement, so two buyers can never both buy the last unit. The price you pay is recorded with the purchase and does not change if the product is repriced later.

For the shop owner

One owner account manages the inventory and sees every sale, filtered by product and date, with a revenue total.
